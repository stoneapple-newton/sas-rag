# PROV-002 - Attach Provenance to Every Emitted Chunk

Type: Feature Story
Parent: [Provenance Schema](../feature-provenance-schema.md)
Capability: Corpus ingestion and provenance
Sprint: Alpha
Points: 5
Status: Done
Dependencies: PROV-001, PARSE-001
Wiki: [Provenance Schema](../../docs/wiki/provenance-schema.md), [Citation Grounding](../../docs/wiki/citation-grounding.md)

## Story

As a retrieval user, I want every chunk to carry provenance, so that search results and answers can identify their SAS source.

## Detail

Wire the required provenance schema into parser and chunker outputs. Provenance must be generated by code, not inferred later by prompts.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Parser and chunker outputs include all required provenance fields.
- Missing required fields fail fast during sample ingestion.
- Chunk IDs are deterministic for unchanged source content and section context.

## Implementation Notes

- Required fields are `chunk_id`, `source_uri`, `title`, `version`, `section_path`, `source_type`, and content hash.
- Preserve provenance through embedding and vector-store writes.
- Include enough metadata for filtering and citation display.

## Done Evidence

- `chunk_unit()` in `src/sas_rag/ingestion/chunker.py` emits all required provenance fields.
- `validate_provenance()` called on every emitted chunk; missing fields raise `ValueError`.
- `stable_chunk_id()` produces deterministic IDs from `(source_id, section_path, ordinal, content_hash)`.
- Sample provenance available in `data/ingestion/runs/latest/chunks.jsonl`.
